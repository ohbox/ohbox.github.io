Hi, I'm Tom Lin — good to meet you!

With 6+ years in UI/UX design, I create user-centered digital products through research, prototyping, and testing with purpose. I'm hands-on from discovery to delivery, detail-oriented yet pragmatic — knowing when to polish and when to ship. I use AI as part of my workflow for research synthesis, inspiration, prompting, rapid prototyping, and light coding, allowing me to explore faster and communicate ideas more clearly. I value design systems, accessibility, and smooth collaboration with developers and product teams from concept to hand-off.

“Good design starts with empathy and ends with impact.”
